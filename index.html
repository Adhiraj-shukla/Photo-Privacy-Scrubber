Photo Privacy Scrubber

See what your photos reveal about you, then remove it. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop photos here, click to choose, or paste with Ctrl+V Several files at once are fine. JPEG, PNG, WebP (HEIC can be read but usually not cleaned)
What to keep

Location and device details are always removed. Keeping anything works for JPEG only; other formats are cleaned completely.

With nothing kept, each photo is redrawn on a canvas, which drops all metadata but re-encodes the image (JPEG and WebP quality 0.95). With options kept, JPEGs are edited without re-encoding.
